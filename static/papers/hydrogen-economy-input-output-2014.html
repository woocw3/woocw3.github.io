What Would a Hydrogen Economy Add to Korea's Output?

Dongphil Chun, Chungwon Woo, Hangyeol Seo, Yanghon Chung, Sungjun Hong, Jongwook Kim (Corresponding author: Chung-Won Woo)
International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, 39(15), 7627-7633, 2014 · DOI: 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2014.03.058

TL;DR — Input–output analysis of Korea's hydrogen energy technology investment for 2020–2040. The hydrogen sector behaves as intermediate primary production — high backward and forward linkage. Total production in the hydrogen sector by 2040 reaches 13,484 or 2,979 billion KRW depending on the scenario. A pioneering economy-wide assessment of Korea's hydrogen industries.

Key findings

1. Hydrogen sits in the middle of the economy. High backward and forward linkage effects characterise the hydrogen sector as intermediate primary production — it both pulls from and feeds into many other sectors.
2. Scale depends heavily on the scenario. By 2040, total production in the hydrogen sector is 13,484 billion KRW under one scenario and 2,979 billion KRW under the other.
3. Context. The government's Hydrogen Energy R&D Center invested about 100 billion KRW between 2003 and 2012; this study is the first to assess the economy-wide effects of that push.

Method in one line

Input–output analysis with a scenario-based exogenous specification method, covering the effect of hydrogen energy technology investment on the Korean economy for 2020–2040.

국문 요약

한국은 에너지 안보 강화를 위해 수소에너지 기술을 개발해 왔으며, 정부가 설립한 수소에너지사업단은 2003~2012년 약 1,000억 원을 투자했다. 본 연구는 산업연관분석(I-O)과 시나리오 기반 외생화 기법으로 수소에너지 기술 투자가 2020~2040년 한국 경제에 미치는 효과를 분석했다. (1) 산업 연관효과와 (2) 수소에너지 공급 투자의 산업별 파급효과 두 관점에 초점을 두었다. 분석 결과 수소 부문은 전후방 연관효과가 모두 높아 중간재적 기초생산 부문으로 특징지어졌다. 2040년까지 수소 부문의 총생산은 두 시나리오에서 각각 13조 4,840억 원과 2조 9,790억 원으로 추정되었다. 한국 수소에너지 산업의 국민경제적 효과를 평가한 선구적 연구다.

FAQ

Q. What do backward and forward linkages mean?
Backward linkage is how much a sector pulls from its suppliers; forward linkage is how much other sectors depend on its output. High values on both mark a sector positioned in the middle of the production structure.

Q. Why do the two scenarios differ so much?
The scenarios apply different assumptions about the scale and pace of hydrogen energy supply investment, so the 2040 production figures bracket a plausible range rather than forecast a single number.

Q. Related work by the authors?
See Chun, Hong, Chung, Woo & Seo (2016, RSER), which evaluates ex-post performance of the same hydrogen R&D programme using DEA.

Full abstract

Korea has been developing hydrogen energy technology to enhance its energy security. The Hydrogen Energy R&D Center established by the Korean government invested about 100 billion Korean won (KRW) into the development of hydrogen energy technology from 2003 to 2012. This study uses input-output (I-O) analysis, along with the scenario-based exogenous specification method, to investigate the effect of hydrogen energy technology investment on the Korean economy for the period 2020-2040. We focus on two perspectives: (1) the sectoral linkage effect and (2) the sectoral impacts of hydrogen energy supply investments. The overall results reveal that the hydrogen sector can be characterized as intermediate primary production because of its high backward and forward linkage effects. By 2040, total production in the hydrogen sector under two scenarios will be 13,484 and 2979 billion KRW, respectively. This study is a pioneering study into the assessment of the economy-wide effects of Korea's hydrogen energy industries.
