Greetings!

Chung-Won Woo 우청원

Associate Professor, Department of Accounting, Changwon National University

I study how carbon regulation, ESG, and technology investment show up in firms' costs, disclosures, audits, and market value. Before joining Changwon National University I was a Research Fellow at the Science and Technology Policy Institute (STEPI), where I led national R&D and science-and-technology policy research for ministries and public agencies.

Research Interests

  • Carbon accounting & climate risk — EU CBAM, carbon risk and audit effort, climate exposure and firm performance
  • ESG & sustainability disclosure — ESG ratings, green supply chains, social enterprise performance
  • Cost behavior & management accounting — cost stickiness, innovation strategy, cost efficiency (DEA)
  • Innovation & R&D performance — national R&D efficiency, open innovation, machine-learning prediction
  • Information security & IT investment — mandatory security disclosure, IT governance, firm value

Published in Journal of Cleaner Production, Technology Analysis & Strategic Management, Sustainable Development, Business Strategy and the Environment, Renewable & Sustainable Energy Reviews, and Korean Accounting Review.
